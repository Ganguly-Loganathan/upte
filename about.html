01 / ABOUT UNIVERSAL POWERTECH ENGINEERING

Practical industrial engineering, built around performance.

Universal PowerTech Engineering brings together industrial electrical, instrumentation, automation, and energy-efficiency solutions for customers across Tamil Nadu and South India.

Our company evolution

Our business roots go back to 1998, when it operated as Universal Marketing Agencies in Rajapalayam. The business evolved into Universal PowerTech Engineering in 2006, expanding our technical footprint with our Chennai office in 2009 and Coimbatore office to serve industrial clusters seamlessly.

We specialize in comprehensive Electrical, Instrumentation, and Automation Products, helping customers evaluate solution-led engineering support under real operating conditions, energy consumption parameters, and safety requirements.

Core Focus Areas

Delivering high-performance industrial electrical solutions and energy optimization backed by leading global manufacturers.

  • ✔ Industrial electrical infrastructure
  • ✔ Power quality & harmonics mitigation
  • ✔ Automation & energy management
  • ✔ Cable support & industrial cooling

02 / OUR JOURNEY

Company Timeline & Milestones

The key milestones that built our reputation in industrial power and automation engineering.

1
1998

Foundation in Rajapalayam

Started our journey initially as Universal Marketing Agencies, building deep technical proficiency in VFDs and specialized industrial electrical service.

2
2004

Technical Community Leadership

Helped establish and lead the "Society of Rajapalayam Electrical Engineers", driving collaborative engineering knowledge sharing across regional industries.

3
2006

Rebranding & Global Partnerships

Formally transitioned into Universal PowerTech Engineering. Secured high-tier partnerships with global brands, earning top channel partner recognition in India.

4
2009 & Beyond

Chennai & Coimbatore Expansion

Established dedicated branch offices and warehouses in Valasaravakkam (Chennai) and Saravanampatti (Coimbatore) to deliver swift, localized technical support throughout South India.

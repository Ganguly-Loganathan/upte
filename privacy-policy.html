Privacy policy

This page explains how information submitted through this website may be handled.

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Information you provide

If you submit an enquiry form, you may provide your name, work email address, phone number, selected area of interest and message. Please do not include passwords, payment card details, government identity numbers or other sensitive personal information.

How information is used

Information submitted through the enquiry form is intended to help Universal PowerTech Engineering respond to your request, clarify technical requirements and communicate about relevant engineering solutions.

Form delivery provider

The website uses FormSubmit as a third-party form delivery service. When you submit a form, the information you enter is transmitted to that service for delivery to the company email address. The service may process information according to its own policies and technical requirements. First-time email verification may be required before delivery is enabled.

Cookies and analytics

This website may load web fonts from Google Fonts and uses standard browser functionality. No analytics or advertising tracking is intentionally configured in this website package. Hosting providers may generate technical logs as part of delivering and securing the site.

Data retention and security

Enquiry information may be retained in the recipient email inbox and relevant business systems for as long as reasonably needed to respond and maintain business records. No website can guarantee absolute security for information transmitted over the internet.

Your choices

You may choose not to submit the form and instead contact the company directly at info@universalpowertechengineering.com. For privacy-related questions, please use that email address.

Third-party websites

Links to manufacturer websites are provided for reference. Those websites operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website or its data practices change. The latest update date will be shown at the top of this page.

Questions about an enquiry?

Contact our team for clarification about the information you submitted.
